Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026 · 中文版本

Table of Contents for Any Article is a browser extension that shows a table of contents for the article on the page you are viewing. This policy explains what information it handles and what it does not do.

Information the extension handles

  • Page content. When you open the extension on a tab, or on a site where you asked it to open by itself, it reads that page's headings and text inside your browser to build the outline and to search it.
  • Scroll position. While the panel is open, it reads how far you have scrolled to highlight the section you are reading.
  • Page address. When you copy the outline with links, the current page's address is used to build those links, and the result is placed on your clipboard.
  • Your settings. Interface language, theme, panel background and opacity, text size, heading levels shown, indentation guides, reading progress, jump offset, the panel's layout (floating, docked or edge rail), position and size, and the list of sites where you chose “Always show on this site” are saved with chrome.storage.local on your device.

All of this stays on your device. None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or browsing data.
  • It makes no network requests and includes no analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It does not run on any page until you open it on that tab, or choose that page's site with “Always show on this site”, and it never reads other tabs.
  • It requires no account.

Permissions

  • activeTab lets the extension access only the tab you open it on, and only after you click its icon or use its keyboard shortcut.
  • scripting lets it add its panel, which is bundled with the extension, to that tab.
  • storage lets it keep your settings on your device.
  • Access to a site is requested only when you turn on “Always show on this site” there (an optional host permission, which Chrome asks you to allow). It lets the panel open by itself on that site. Turning the option off gives the permission back, and you can also revoke it on the extension's page in Chrome.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes its saved settings. Closing the panel or reloading the page removes everything else it held.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 26 日

「网页文章目录」是一款浏览器扩展，用于为你正在浏览的网页文章生成目录。本政策说明它会处理哪些信息，以及它不会做什么。

扩展会处理的信息

  • 网页内容。当你在某个标签页中打开本扩展，或在你设为"在本站自动显示"的站点上浏览时，它会在你的浏览器内读取该页面的标题和文字，用来生成目录和搜索。
  • 滚动位置。面板打开期间，它会读取页面的滚动位置，用来高亮你正在阅读的章节。
  • 网页地址。当你复制带链接的目录时，会用当前网页的地址生成这些链接，结果放入你的剪贴板。
  • 你的设置。界面语言、主题、面板背景和透明度、字号、显示层级、缩进参考线、阅读进度开关、跳转留白、面板的布局（浮动、停靠或迷你导航条）、位置和大小，以及你设为"在本站自动显示"的站点列表，通过 chrome.storage.local 保存在你的设备上。

以上信息全部保留在你的设备上，不会发送给开发者或任何第三方。

扩展不会做的事

  • 不收集、不传输、不出售、不分享任何个人信息或浏览数据。
  • 不发起任何网络请求，不包含任何统计、追踪或广告代码。
  • 在你于某个标签页中打开它、或把该站点设为"在本站自动显示"之前，它不会在该页面运行，也从不读取其他标签页。
  • 不需要注册账号。

权限说明

  • activeTab：只允许本扩展访问你打开它的那个标签页，并且只在你点击图标或使用快捷键之后。
  • scripting：把扩展自带的目录面板添加到该标签页。
  • storage：把你的设置保存在你的设备上。
  • 站点访问权限：只在你于某个站点开启"在本站自动显示"时申请（可选的主机权限，由 Chrome 征求你的同意），用于让目录在该站点自动打开。关闭该选项会交还这项权限，你也可以在 Chrome 的扩展页面上撤销它。

删除数据

卸载本扩展会删除它保存的设置。关闭面板或刷新页面后，它持有的其他信息也会随之清除。

政策变更

如本政策有更新，新版本会发布在同一地址，并注明新的生效日期。
